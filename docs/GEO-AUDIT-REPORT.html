Executive Summary

St. Louis Creations has a crawlable, production-ready Next.js site with a live sitemap, clear public routes, broad crawler access, live llms.txt, canonical links, JSON-LD schema, and improved AI-citable content blocks. The June 2 implementation resolved the earlier machine-interpretation gaps around schema, canonical clarity, llms.txt availability, and homepage answer extraction. The June 3, 2026 content sprint added five core topic hubs, FAQ proof pages, use-case proof pages, B2B laser engraving and 3D printing blog posts, consumer 3D printing content, and an expanded llms.txt. The latest production evidence sprint adds two source-driven evidence blocks to each core topic hub with 120-180 word answer sections, concrete quantity examples, process constraints, material limits, and quote inputs. The latest production sprint expands that to four evidence blocks per core hub and adds semantic comparison tables for decoration methods, 3D printing processes, 3D printing materials, process selection, and engraving materials.

The practical priority remains making the site easy for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google Search, and Google AI Overview systems to understand as a named local fabrication business. A 100/100 quick audit score means the technical GEO/SEO readiness checks are strong; it does not guarantee organic rankings, AI citations, or Google AI Overview inclusion. The separate citability scorer remains conservative because it rewards true statistics, original measured data, sourced proof, and question-style standalone passages more than schema or internal-link context. The production hub pages now produce multiple B-grade best blocks after deployment dpl_CHaTaWLkCnsDHM3Ra9MY1mBHmP42. The new comparison tables are visible in production-rendered HTML and contribute additional cite-ready decision criteria on the five core topic hubs.
